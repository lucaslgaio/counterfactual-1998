## Growth-cap status

The Etapa-4 safety cap (`MAX_POSITIVE_GROWTH_RATIO = 0.015`) was kept in
place — see [issue #13](../../issues). After calibration, removing the
cap caused 7 metrics (mostly `science_rd.*`, gini, and disinfo) to grow
more than 5× initial in a 58-turn big-bang run, because their edges
weren't constrained by data.

`scripts/remove_growth_cap.py` correctly detected the explosions and
auto-restored the backup. The path forward is to source historical data
for those metrics and re-run calibration.
